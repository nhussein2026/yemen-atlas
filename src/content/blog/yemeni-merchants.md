  en: "In the heart of ancient Yemen lay the Kingdom of Saba, a civilization that captured the imagination of the ancient world. Known for its wealth, advanced engineering, and legendary Queen, Saba was not just a regional power but a global economic force. The story of Saba is the story of how Yemen's merchants and engineers created one of history's most sophisticated societies.\n\n## The Rise of Saba\n\nThe Kingdom of Saba (often associated with the biblical Sheba) emerged in the early 1st millennium BCE. Its capital, **Marib**, was strategically located on the edge of the Arabian desert, serving as a gateway between the fertile highlands and the desert trade routes.\n\nSaba's power rested on two pillars: **agricultural innovation** and **monopoly over the incense trade**. By mastering water management in an arid environment and controlling the production of frankincense and myrrh, the Sabaeans built an empire that lasted for over a thousand years."
